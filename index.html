Build your own eye tracking for VR.

EyeTrackVR is an open-source eye tracker you build yourself. A small camera for each eye fits inside your headset, so your avatar's eyes move with yours in VRChat and other social VR apps.

Start with the introductionSee the parts list

How it works

  1. 1

    Cameras watch your eyes

    A small camera for each eye sits inside the headset, lit by low-power infrared LEDs.

  2. 2

    Your PC does the tracking

    The cameras stream over USB or Wi-Fi to the EyeTrackVR app, which works out where you are looking and how open your eyes are.

  3. 3

    Your avatar looks where you look

    The app sends the result over OSC to VRChat, or anything else that speaks OSC.

Before you start

More answers in the FAQ.

Will it fit my headset?
Very likely. It works with any headset the hardware fits inside, using a community mount or one you design yourself. See the mounts
Is infrared light safe for my eyes?
Infrared can harm your eyes above a certain power. The official kits are designed around published exposure limits, so read the safety page before you power anything and never bypass a safety feature. Read LED safety
What does it cost?
It depends on the boards and cameras you pick. The parts list prices every option. Open the parts list
Where do my eye images go?
Nowhere. The app runs on your own PC and collects no data.
